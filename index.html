        Sources and definitions

        Carried over counts open requests at fiscal year-end. Overdue counts the carried-over requests beyond the legislated timeline. The within-timeline rate uses closed requests as the denominator and includes extensions allowed by law. It is unavailable when no requests were closed.

        Counts describe whole institutions and fiscal years. ATI and Privacy Act figures describe separate workloads. Missing reports and renamed institutions can interrupt a series. Choose “Exclude IRCC” to compare results with Treasury Board reports that analyze Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada separately.

        The research workspace uses separate disclosure registers. Reporting months and dates received control its date filters. The underlying disclosures cover published ATI summaries and briefing titles.
